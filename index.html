AI Governance & Technology Policy

MPA from UC Berkeley's Goldman School of Public Policy. I study how governance systems are designed, who they serve, and what happens when they break.

About

Eight years across UN agencies, international NGOs, venture capital, tech, and academia — each context sharpening the same question: who gets to shape the rules of emerging technologies, and who bears the cost when those rules fail?

I bring a cross-sector lens to AI governance, grounded in international development practice and trained in the institutional mechanics of policy design. Trilingual in English, Spanish, and German. Dual US/EU work eligibility.
